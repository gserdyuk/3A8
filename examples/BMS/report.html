The readings, on one scale

Four distributions, all in net working time and all at the same scope: three independent readings of the outside view, and the calibrated bottom-up. The widest disagreement here is not between the two methods — it is between the three readings of the same one.

Obligations · 73 Contradictions · 4 Differing readings · 10 Defaults taken · 8

The three outside-view readings are not three repeats. A and B are repeats of one another — same input, same day, 22 August. C was made on 25 August with one assumption withheld and an additional question asked, so part of the distance between C and the pair may be that difference rather than the sensor's own spread. All three were produced without ever seeing the product model, the work items or the rate table.

Only the marked quantiles are readings; the curve between them is interpolation. A and B were asked for P10, P50, P80, P90 and a floor, and stop there — their tails are hatched rather than drawn, because inventing a P95 is the move this method exists to prevent. C was asked for the tail and answered: P95 = 3450 days, P99 = 6375 and off this scale. It declined to give an unconditional P99 at all, on the ground that beyond a point the project is stopped and restarted and effort stops being the right unit for what happened.

Declared premise. Time in an estimate by analogy contains the losses to meetings, and is already clean of leave and days off. All three readings confirmed this of themselves in writing — each reasons in assigned working days and each flagged the mismatch with the unit label it was given. Their own conversions to strict 8-hour task-days: A ×0.8 · B ×0.75–0.85 · C ×0.56–0.75. The declared house factor 0.75 is applied to all three, and the bottom-up needs no conversion because it was priced in task hours to begin with.

The answer

Person-days of 8 net hours each — work content, and nothing else. No calendar, no headcount and no money is implied by these figures.

The disagreement, diagnosed rather than averaged

At equal scope the calibrated bottom-up is ×1.49 to ×3.17 above the outside-view medians, and sits at ~P75 of one reading and above P90 of the other two. Steps B–D attributed the gap without closing it, and reported the explained share as 0% in those words.

against itself
The largest single quantity in the comparison ×2.12
The three outside-view medians span 600 to 1275 net days — wider than the distance from the bottom-up to any one of them. Before any unit conversion the raw medians are 800, 1050 and 1700 recorded days, so the conversion did not create it. Part of it may be reading C's changed input, and n = 1 on that input cannot separate the two.
D1
The recursive assembly convention
Integration priced at 20% of each subtree's leaf effort at all 18 parents — which delivers 40.9% of leaf effort, because a deep leaf enters every ancestor's base. It is the method's only free constant. And it cannot carry the gap: deleting it entirely still leaves the calibrated figure ×1.08 to ×2.29 above the outside-view medians.
D4
Class assignment
Every reading flagged its own most likely error as upward. Reading C put 60% on the stated class and split the remaining 40% asymmetrically — 24 points move the answer up, 16 down, and the up-moves are larger. Misclassification adds right-skew on top of the class's own.
D6
Unit provenance settled
Net versus recorded time, carried as two forks and applied in neither direction. Resolved 25 August, and it resolved against the bottom-up: the chain prices task-solving hours, the outside view reasons from recorded project time and converts at 0.75, and the gap widened rather than closing. Leave is deliberately not deducted — COCOMO's 152-hour person-month and every timesheet-based dataset removed it already.
—
Explained share: 0%, stated in those words
Every correction the gap-blind rate step proposed was an upward hole-fill. False convergence was checked in three frames and refused in all three — including a near-match within 3%, flagged as exactly what a unit artefact looks like when it imitates agreement. The methods have not converged, and the report calls that the correct outcome, not a defect.

What the document contradicts itself about

Produced without being asked for, by instruments whose job was something else, and converged on across six independent runs. On the record this is the part that reproduces best.

R29 / R38
A direct contradiction largest basis risk
R29 requires automatic booking. R38 says hotel supplier booking is handled manually. Both estimator pairs, independently, named this their largest risk of basis. Reading taken: automatic where an integration exists, manual elsewhere, failure falls back to the manual path.
R05
An actor list that omits an actor it gives work to
The stated actors are the travel department, transport suppliers and employees — while R38 assigns booking work to hotel suppliers, who appear nowhere in the list.
R13 / R14
Two obligations with nothing measurable in them
Availability and volume obligations that name no target. Nothing verifies them, because the document states no number to verify against.
MD
Three model defects, converged on by independent instruments
The audit-trail store declares things it never names · “clear business processes” names no component that could realise it · two statements carry unmeasurable non-functional requirements. All three surfaced as refusals — work the sensors judged necessary and declined to invent — and belong on the model owner's desk.

Where the document reads two ways

Pinned before any run and consumed by every run. The procedure: ask the client; if no answer comes, assume; declare the assumption; and when runs are compared afterwards, exclude the differences the open question causes. All ten stand unasked here, because this document has no client behind it — which is precisely why the assumptions must be visible. In a live engagement the first move is to send this list.

The defaults taken, and what each is worth

Each is a fork the document leaves open and the estimate had to close. The reading taken is inside the centre; the reading refused is priced separately and enters only when its trigger fires. Nothing is struck out — an obligation can be bounded, never deleted.

A4
Supplier search worth an order of magnitude
Taken: 1–2 aggregator APIs. Refused: dozens of direct hotel integrations. If reversed: 12.8–51.6 d per integration plus 45–149 once — at N = 20 that is +301 to +1181 days on a centre of 1899.
A5
SMS
Taken: a ready gateway. Refused: own SMS infrastructure and carrier agreements. If reversed: +60 to +239 d, less the gateway adapter.
A8
“Major disruption situations”
Taken: technical resilience and disaster recovery. Refused: business mass re-booking during a strike or a volcano. If reversed: +112 to +288 d — and the sensor that flagged it called the refused reading the largest single omission in this model.
A9
“Admin and Support”
Named in a diagram, given content nowhere. Assumed: an internal support console. Not assumed: a ticketing product, a help desk, a knowledge base, an SLA engine.
scope
×4

Staged UAT rather than direct client acceptance · dev/stage/prod rather than another environment count · seeded data rather than migration · operational and user documentation rather than operational only. Each a declared fork, each separately priceable.
term

Hosting, support and the technology-currency programme are carried and not priced, because an open-ended obligation has no effort figure until somebody says for how long. That is a question, not an assumption to make.

What is in no number, by name

Not oversights. Each is a thing the chain refused to price, with the reason it refused.

1
The continuing service awaiting a parameter
Running the hosted system, operating support, periodic technology reviews. Carried on both sides, awaiting the term. Priced by a rate per unit time once a term exists — a different instrument.
2
The four refused readings priced as options
Each is a step change, not a multiplier: not in the centre, priced above, entering only when its trigger fires.
3
Three coverage verifications the rate agent returned
Is privacy work actually carried by the 68 obligations · is the front-end baseline responsive or desktop-only · is there truly no legacy data to migrate. Any “no” becomes a new named hole, never a silent uplift.
4
The structure axis
A second product model of the same requirements differs ×1.65 in node count and was never priced. Unquantified width, named as such.
5
Effort to calendar, and team availability
A separate step, deliberately. Person-days do not become dates without a team shape, and this report supplies no team shape.

Where every number came from

Each sensor is an engine with a version, stamped on its own output. They are hired for what they are forbidden to see, not for autonomy.

1
Product model
73 pinned obligations → 78 elements. Produces no effort figure of any kind.
2
Work model
Crossed against a declared technology → 553 work items. May not invent an activity: work the product needs but no declared activity covers is reported as a finding.
3
Size classes
Each element classified by counting named things, the enumeration being the justification. Refuses to run if shown any rate or price. Repeat agreement ×1.008 on the total.
4
Rate table uncalibrated
External norms, gap-blind: never saw a project total, a budget, or a gap a rate would explain. Calibrated against no outcome yet — the centre is norms passed through a measured size vector, not a validated cost.
5
Outside view ×3
Ran in parallel and in ignorance of all of the above. Every reading declined to read the repository whose path it could see, each naming anchoring as the reason.
6
Steps B, C, D
Calibration parameters from a gap-blind source that never saw the outside view or the size of the gap; then the diagnosis, the only participant permitted to see more than one method's output.

What this report is not. It is not a validated price, and it is not a price at all — it is work content in person-days, and converting it into money, calendar or headcount is the reader's act, using the reader's own figures. Every bottom-up number rests on a rate table of external industry norms calibrated against no outcome; the bottom-up stands on one product model of two; and this is a training document with no outcome, so nothing here has been checked against reality.

What it is. The estimate, its corridor, the disagreement between two structurally independent methods stated rather than averaged away, and — on the record, the part that reproduces best — the obligations, the contradictions, the questions and the defaults that the number is standing on.
