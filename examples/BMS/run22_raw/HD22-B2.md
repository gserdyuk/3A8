## Closure violations — eight, none priced

The run drew a distinction repeat 1 did not: **"a declared boundary is not a violation"** — the
whole-project activities the task declared out of scope are not listed, only what is visible as missing
*from inside* the six subtrees.

1. **CV1 — no UAT for the integration, search and workflow subtrees.** "R19 puts custom prioritisation
   rules in the Travel Manager's own hands and R25 defines an approval process the client operates —
   these are precisely what a client accepts or rejects."
2. **CV2 — no surface for defining custom prioritisation rules (R19).** "A rule engine without a way to
   author rules does not realise R19… It may be hiding inside K2-N72 unstated, which is why K2-N72-b
   carries the widest O–P spread in the model."
3. **CV3 — nothing uploads the manually uploaded bookings that R16 searches.**
4. **CV4 — no store for bookings or booking requirements.** "K2-N38, K2-N42, K2-N85-c and K2-N34 are
   priced *assuming* that store exists."
5. **CV5 — no performance testing beneath the booking subtrees.** R66 is measured once, at the UX
   statement; "the hotel and transport surfaces are where a Travel Manager actually waits."
6. **CV6 — nothing prices the six subtrees meeting each other**, because the root is not an element and
   C3 fires nowhere above them. "That assembly — the largest one in the system — has no item and no C3
   base."
7. **CV7 — no design activity on the aggregates.** "C3 prices the *cost* of the parts meeting; it does
   not produce the design that makes them meet."
8. **CV8 — no seed or reference data outside journey locations** — suppliers, users, roles.

3. **A8-N30 (5.50), the search corpus** — "the most consequential test-data item in the model and the

4. **N44 combination rules (20.83)** — "R45 is six words."
5. **Six independent regression suites (33.32)** almost certainly share harness work. "I have not
   reduced them: I may not merge."
6. **Two cycles and no third.** "If cycle 2 leaves defects, this model has nowhere to price cycle 3, and
   that is a property of the work model, not of my numbers."
7. **The C3 base reading moves a real amount.** Under the narrower reading — children's leaves only —
   integration falls from **148.34 to 101.92** and the total from **890.09 to 843.67**. "Whoever
   calibrates against this reading must know which base was used."
8. **N16 is class `statement` yet has two `surface` children**, and draws subtree-level test, UAT and
   documentation as if it were an aggregate.
