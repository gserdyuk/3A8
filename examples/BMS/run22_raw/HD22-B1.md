- **D-1 — N37 Automatic Hotel Booking may be empty work (22.49 pd).** R38 says hotel supplier booking is
  manual and the system performs no external automation, and no hotel-booking interface exists — N22 is
  a content aggregator covering search only. "If R38 governs, most of this 22.49 is not work. If R29
  governs and the aggregator books hotels too, K2-N37 is badly undersized. I cannot resolve this and
  must not."
- **D-2 — R29 is covered three times** (N73, N37, N41 — 84.48 pd together); the boundary between
  orchestration and the automatic paths is not drawn in the input.
- **D-4 — the second-cycle assumption has the widest leverage in the estimate.** Every `-c2` item was
  sized at 0.6–0.7 of its `-c1` twin. "Ten `-c2` items across six parents ride on that reading… this
  single assumption moves more of the total than any individual item does."
- **D-6 — six regression suites and no suite above them**: only one names a harness explicitly, so the
  other five carry an implicit share of the same scaffolding — "almost certainly double-counts harness
  work".
- Also: D-3 (N84 and N73 both define what failure means), D-5 (one aggregator or two), D-7 (load
  reconciliation possibly redundant against the frequent sync).
