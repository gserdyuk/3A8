## The findings, in one place

1. **The sign is inverted**: the table-priced bottom-up sits at ≈P66–P82 of the class *before* any
   correction, and the whole calibrated corridor sits at or above class P80. The gap decomposes
   directionally into **D1** (the recursive assembly convention — root C3 alone is 33–62% of the
   face-value gap), **D4** (class weight, the RC's own upward-flagged error mode), **D6** (unit
   provenance, a ×1.25 lever) — none priceable by any supplied rate.
2. **Explained share 0%** — the second time in project history the diagnostic step has gone red, and
   the first where the red is structural rather than an instrument failure: gap-blind hole-fills
   cannot close a gap that runs the other way. The report says so plainly instead of manufacturing
   convergence.
3. **No reserve headroom** — with the centre at the class P90, the tail events (the four refused
   readings) are covered by nothing. The bid either adds a gap-blind step-event sizing round (U5) or
   states tail-lessness on its face.
4. **Two facts move everything**, both single questions: the rate table's unit provenance (±≈475 pd
   on the centre) and the class-assignment weight. Both are adjudications, not estimates.
