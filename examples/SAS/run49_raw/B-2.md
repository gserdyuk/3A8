**Reconciliation.** The bottom-up figure carries double-count risk in three places — the per-feature lines and the separate UX and requirements lines overlap, the NFR block overlaps the per-feature and QA blocks, and the two modules share far more mechanism than separate sizing implies (A15 is explicit that publish/subscribe is one mechanism seen from two sides; A10 makes industry variation configuration, not code paths). Correcting for that pulls the chain toward the upper reference band rather than above it. I settle at **250 pm**, above the reference-class midpoint because the reference class rarely carries both a near-real-time read store and an 11M-record migration, and because the business rules to be validated against do not yet exist when the project starts.
