**Reference-class check.** A three-module B2B replacement of this NFR weight, in a standards domain the team has not built in before (A3), with business rules still to be defined, lands empirically at 15–20 FTE over 16–20 calendar months. At 20 FTE × 18 months that is ~360 calendar person-months; stripping leave, holidays and sickness (~15%) gives ~300 pm on this convention. The reference class sits above the bottom-up, which is the usual direction — the bottom-up has no line for the rework that domain novelty and undefined standards generate.

**Reconciliation.** I take the bottom-up as the floor of the plausible band rather than the centre, and settle between the two: the decomposition is credible element by element but systematically under-prices integration, the second and third passes over validation rules, and the cost of meeting the response times against a permission model that filters every query. Centre ~240 pm. The spread is wide on purpose: the single largest unpinned driver is the depth of the publish/subscribe permission model (record-level, group-level, hierarchy-level, with approval) interacting with search performance, and a bad reading there is worth ±60 pm on its own.
