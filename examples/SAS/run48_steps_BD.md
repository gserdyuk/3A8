## Registered expectations

1. **Stamps on everything**: the output opens with its own stamp and records the full input chain
   (`Hotyn-M 1.1 / Hotyn-W 1.1 / Hotyn-D 2.0` × table v0.1-h · `Lytin-R 1.1` ×2 · `Lytin-K 1.1`).
2. **The declarations are reconciled before anything is attributed**: three units (net task hours;
   168 recorded h per PM with leave outside; 152 charged h per PM with leave inside) put into one,
   with the conversion applied to the class side only and the units component of the gap sized first.
   The chain-vs-class ratio must not be quoted before that reconciliation.
3. **No averaging anywhere**: the two RC readings stay a band (their P50s are ×1.77 apart raw); the two
   repeats stay a band; centre and tail never mixed.
4. **The chain is applied mechanically** in the fixed order (T1, T2, T3 on their bases; 20% on the
   increments; A-1…A-5 flat; G2; G1), shown with running totals, low / central / high, both repeats;
   no rate altered, added or dropped; uncovered spots answered by naming, not by filling.
5. **The final answer arrives in three parts** — centre (calibrated bottom-up), corridor (spread of
   the calibration), reserve (the raw class tail, both readings, untouched) — plus the explained
   share, the residual, and the false-convergence check.
6. **The four sensor-flagged cross-cutting issues are addressed, not absorbed**: the unit
   disagreement between the two class readings; the seam with the carried post-production period
   (both RC readings warned of it); the era mismatch both refused to adjust for; the rate agent's
   tail hand-off list (discrete branches, "another sensor's tail").

## The findings, in one place

1. **The dominant uncertainty is inside the outside view, not between the views.** The chain sits
   ×0.85 of one class median and ×1.56 of the other; the two class medians are ×1.82 apart after
   conversion, and units explain 4–7% of that. Attributed to **one free parameter closed two ways**:
   the functional-size regime of the ISBSG anchor — RC46-2 closed it with a number (1 200–3 000
   units × ~12 h), RC46-1 with words (">1 000 FP") — plus a staffing anchor about two different
   crews, plus misclassification leaning in opposite directions at equal 60% confidence.
2. **Calibration is one-directional and therefore cannot meet both readings.** All Step C corrections
   point up (the bottom-up's known systematic is omission). Result: the calibrated centre, 57 600 h,
   lands at ≈P63 of RC46-1 and **above P90 of RC46-2**. The diagnostician names the trilemma —
   the chain overshoots, or RC46-2's level is low, or its size regime is wrong — and declines to
   resolve it, which is its definition.
3. **The rate table's level is the one uncovered spot that acts multiplicatively on the whole
   centre.** `Lytin-K 1.1` spent both permitted globals on sourced effects and refused to touch the
   table; the diagnostician asks for a narrow gap-blind round on exactly that question and says the
   centre should be read as "the table's level ×1.50" until it runs.
4. **Two facts move everything**: a single declared functional size for the first release, from an
   instrument blind to both class figures, with `Lytin-R` re-run on it (expected to collapse the
   class-vs-class ×1.82 to ~×1.2); and the external check of table v0.1-h. The third, the W-4 term,
   moves no number here but is the client question the log already words.

## Two instrument findings the run returned unasked

- **The bottom-up declares no roles.** The assembly output states unit and losses but not whose work
  is counted; the diagnostician inferred the role coverage from the activity composition and flagged
  that the inference is not a declaration. **Instrument debt:** the assembly script's unit line gains
  a roles line (every vendor role the catalogue's activities imply; client-side effort excluded).
- **245 elements vs 187 sized elements.** The coverage report handed to Step C says "1 650 items over
  245 elements"; the assembly says 187 sized. Both are right — 245 is every element of the work model
  including the 58 parents, 187 the sizeable leaves — but the brief did not say so and the
  diagnostician correctly refused to reconcile it. **Brief debt:** state both counts and their
  relation in the coverage report.
