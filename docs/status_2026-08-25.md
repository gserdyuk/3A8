## 0. The unit, first, because nothing below means anything without it

**Settled 2026-08-25, by measurement.** Two gap-blind `Hotyn-K 1.0` × Fable 5 runs priced 24 cells
**directly in person-hours**. Both stated their own convention unprompted — *one person-day = 8 net
hours of work on the task* — matching the table's original §2 and §5 text. Both, asked separately
afterwards, put a real working day's yield at **5–6 net task hours**. Three independent runs, one
answer, and two distinct quantities that had been riding in one sentence:

- **8 net hours** is what a rate-table person-day contains. It is the table's denomination.
- **5–6 net hours** is what an assigned working day of this team grade delivers. It converts effort
  into calendar or staffing, belongs to the estimator, and never to a rate row.

The 2026-08-22 relabelling ("one person-day = one assigned working day") is **withdrawn**; no value
changed. The eleven case-specific A2/A3 addendum rows, written in assigned days, are converted:
117.33 assigned-days = 704 net person-hours = **88.0 table pd**.

**Recorded because it cost a run and should not be repeated:** the *designed* test — the ratio of
re-derived hours to table person-days — **failed**. Within a single run it ranges 3.0 to 11.4,
because it is dominated by magnitude noise, not by the unit. What answered the question was the
runs' direct statements, which the noise does not touch.

### 1a. The rate table is a fixed set of constants

`docs/rate_table.md` is one gap-blind sample from `Hotyn-K 1.0` × Fable 5, 2026-08-21, and the
author's decision of 2026-08-25 is that it **stays that sample**: not averaged, not re-derived, not
re-sampled. It changes only by calibration against documented outcomes, producing v0.2 with the
evidence recorded beside each changed value. The reason is comparability — every priced run to date
(BMS 23–25, FaxRxTx 29–31) used these constants.

Measured in passing on 2026-08-25: two runs of that role on an identical prompt differ **×1.38 on
the sum of 24 cells, ×1.50 on the median cell**, agreeing exactly on 3 of 24 — conventions agreed
perfectly, magnitudes did not. Under the decision this is **provenance, not the dispersion of any
estimate**: a constant does not vary between runs. The proposal to build the chain's missing
corridor out of that spread is **withdrawn**.

## 5. Readings — what may be claimed

Each traces to a recorded reading; none is introduced here.

**5.1 — The variance programme is finished and it succeeded on its own terms.** Externalising the
magnitude into a fixed table took repeat-run disagreement on the total from ×1.288 to ×1.032, on
two cases. *Overturned by:* a third case where table-priced repeats disagree by more than ×1.10.

**5.2 — One case passes, and case 1 sets the floor, not the standard.** The author's decision of
2026-08-25 admits a comparison whose unit conventions arrived after the estimate — *"an explanation
after the fact is a poor explanation, but it is still an explanation."* By that same wording, cases
2–4 are held to the stricter rule: the case profile pinned **before any estimate exists**. Otherwise
the gate measures how well a case's conditions can be reconstructed afterwards rather than how well
the instrument estimates.

**5.3 — The pass survives a known defect, and that is the informative half.** The chain lands at
×1.21 while under-pricing one stage by ×8.5. A defect of that shape is invisible in a total and
lethal inside it — which is exactly what a rate card exists to make findable.

**5.4 — The pipeline is better at finding missing scope than at producing numbers.** Across six runs
and two instruments it converged on the same seven holes in the BMS product model; on FaxRxTx it
named 5 unsizeable elements and 13 closure violations *before any fact was opened*. For presale that
list is half the value of a deliverable, and no `Lytin` artifact produced it. *Overturned by:* a
case where the named holes turn out not to be holes.

**5.5 — What the method has built is a size-measurement instrument.** A reproducible count of work
items by (element class × activity), traceable to requirements, with a coverage matrix and gap list
attached — a function-point analogue derived from the RFP. Its unit price is the half that is fixed
by decision and calibrated by nothing yet.

**5.6 — The transferable finding of the whole episode.** **The unit of a comparison is part of the
comparison, and it is an input on both sides.** Three failures in three days, all the same shape — a
convention asserted rather than checked: the fact recorded as headcount × calendar and read as
delivered work; a conversion applied to one side only, worth ×1.33 in the estimator's own favour;
and the table's hour content relabelled without re-derivation, a relabelling its own author declined
to certify. The method held all three simultaneously without noticing.

## 6. Working method, changed 2026-08-25

The author named the pattern: a fact is checked, an incidental observation is promoted to a finding
in the same turn, written up, and made the basis of the next turn — and then turns out not to be
what it was called. Four rules, adopted:

1. **One question per run**, written down before launch: what is asked, and what would answer it.
   The answer comes first in the report.
2. **By-products are parked, not promoted.** One line in a parking list. **A by-product may not
   change a conclusion in the same turn it appeared.** It becomes work only if the author takes it
   up, or on its third recurrence.
3. **No document is written without the author's word.** Exception: raw run transcripts, which are
   the protocol and are cheap.
4. **"Nothing came of it" is a legitimate result.** Treating every run as owing a finding is what
   manufactures the spiral.
